# Product

Automatically generate a standard software-engineering CV from user-provided content. The user supplies the career facts, selects relevant material and writes the claims; the application organises and renders the selected content faithfully.

## Standard CV structure

- Default to a familiar, single-column, top-to-bottom layout with conventional section headings.
- Make employers, roles, dates and relevant technologies easy to find. Keep each entry and its supporting bullets clearly grouped.
- Allow section ordering to reflect the applicant's strongest evidence: work experience normally leads for experienced applicants; education, internships or substantive projects may be more prominent early in a career.
- Support concise accomplishment bullets for experience and projects, and compact lists of relevant languages and technologies.
- Keep contact details and useful professional/project links readable and usable.
- Use consistent, readable typography, spacing and alignment. Prefer concise content over cramped presentation; additional pages can be justified by relevant material and the applicant's context.
- A summary is optional and should add useful context rather than boilerplate.

## Export goals

Document accessibility and verified ATS compatibility are future goals, not established capabilities. Accessibility requirements and current limitations are in [Accessibility](accessibility.md).

ATS means Applicant Tracking Systems. ATS-friendly output should expose readable text and recognisable sections while preserving the associations between names, contact details, employers, roles, dates, education and skills. Compatibility requires evidence from the relevant parsing workflow; it is not a promise of automated acceptance or an interview.

## Preview/export parity

Standard visual previews must match the exported PDF's selected content, typography, geometry, line wrapping and pagination. Pixel-level edge smoothing may differ between viewers and displays; substituted fonts, shifted content or different page breaks are not equivalent.
